NEXUS

AI-Powered Criminal Network Analysis System

NEXUS is an AI-powered criminal network analysis platform designed to transform fragmented investigation data into connected intelligence. It allows investigators to ingest documents and extract important entities such as people, organizations, locations, and other relevant information. The platform cleans, normalizes, validates, and deduplicates the extracted data before connecting related entities into an interactive intelligence network.

NEXUS provides relationship mapping, interactive 3D network visualization, timeline analysis, and geo-analysis to help users understand connections between entities and events. It also includes a Data Quality & Intelligence Engine that improves the reliability and consistency of investigation data.

Features

  • AI-powered entity extraction
  • Entity deduplication and normalization
  • Relationship mapping
  • Interactive 3D network visualization
  • Timeline analysis
  • Geo-analysis
  • Document ingestion and processing
  • Data quality analysis
  • Investigation intelligence dashboard

SCAM DETECTOR

AI-Powered Scam & Fraud Detection Platform

Scam Detector is an AI-powered platform designed to analyze suspicious documents and information to identify potential scams and fraudulent activity. The system processes submitted content and generates a structured risk assessment containing a risk score, confidence level, classification, and an executive summary.

The platform is designed to make scam analysis easier to understand by providing explainable results instead of simply returning a prediction. It can also retrieve and display relevant company information, helping users investigate organizations associated with suspicious documents or claims.
